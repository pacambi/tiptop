Tip Top support

Tip Top, shown as Tourenplan Exporter in early beta builds, is an independent Safari extension for exporting your own Tourenplan schedule as JSON or CSV.

Enable Tourenplan Exporter in Safari's extension settings. Open the extension from Safari's page menu. Choose Try demo to use fictional schedules without an SBB account.

For live exports, allow the requested Tourenplan website access, log into Tourenplan normally, reload the page, and open a day before extracting. Keep the panel open until the export finishes.

Contact pa@cambi.ch. Include your device model, OS version, app version/build, and steps to reproduce. Do not send passwords, authentication headers, or unredacted schedules.
